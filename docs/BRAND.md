# Wonder Why Kit — brand and voice

- **Name:** Wonder Why Kit
- **Tagline:** Open. Play. Wonder.
- **Mission line:** For every child who wonders.
- **Chosen domain:** wonderwhykit.org. Availability was checked; registration and deployment are not confirmed.
- **Audience:** children aged 8–12, with families, tutors and volunteers supporting their play.

## Lead with play

Invite children to open, play, change, notice, wonder and try again. Prioritise engagement, agency, humour and joy. Understanding follows the experience; it is not an entry requirement or a test to pass.

Do not use “science” or “experiments” in public-facing promotional copy, headlines, kit invitations or video introductions. Keep the underlying concepts and pedagogy in the background, available in explanations and tutor or maintainer resources when useful. Preserve accurate explanations; avoid leading with technical vocabulary or classroom framing.

## Use across materials

Use “Wonder Why Kit” with “Open. Play. Wonder.” on the collection homepage, packaging and video end cards. Use “For every child who wonders.” for mission statements and community or fundraising materials. Individual activities keep their own titles, such as “Helicopter Seeds”, with a Wonder Why Kit credit.

Example invitation: “Change the breeze. Drop a seed. What happens next?”

Example short description: “Playful kits and little worlds to explore, change and enjoy.”

Keep activity paths stable when the umbrella brand changes. Do not present the chosen domain as a live site until registration and hosting are verified.

## Free kits

Kits will be free and cannot be purchased. Sponsorship is opening soon; do not show active purchase, donation or sponsorship payment controls. The About page holds nonprofit setup details and must not claim registered status before it is confirmed.

## Website assets

The open-kit/question-mark logo is an editable SVG recreation of the approved concept direction: `site/assets/logo.svg`. A one-colour version and simplified question-mark favicon are alongside it. Keep the full mark beside the name; use the question mark alone only at favicon size.

Website palette: forest `#304f40`, cream `#faf6ed`, sky `#a7c8d8`, peach `#e9a581`, butter `#ffda86`, with sage accents. Rounded bold headings and a readable humanist body follow the sheet; system font fallbacks are used, so letterforms vary by device. The generated sheet did not identify an actual font.

Use gentle tactile shading and handwritten accents sparingly. Keep the agreed tagline and mission line; decorative concept-sheet slogans are not required copy.

## Physical play first

Wonder Why Kit is first and foremost a physical kit. Curated, carefully chosen materials invite children to engage with the world around them. Most time should be spent with the kit, not on the website. Web activities, guides and videos support physical play rather than replace it. Lead with this in homepage and About copy. Maintain public tutor and privacy copy directly in `site/tutors.html` and `site/privacy.html`; there are no Markdown duplicates.
