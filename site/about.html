Our story

Start with a smile.

Wonder Why Kit began with a simple dream: a small kit in every child’s hands, in every country, on every continent.

A bubble. A feather. A paper loop that does something unexpected. The invitation is to play, change something, notice what happens and try again.

Wonder Why Kit is first and foremost a physical kit. We carefully choose materials that invite children to touch, change, build and explore the world around them. We want children to spend most of their time with the kit, away from a screen.

Wonder in your hands

Free to receive.
Made to share.

Our planned physical kits will contain a few simple materials and a playful guide, small enough to carry and share with a friend.

Kits will be free for children and cannot be purchased. We plan to work with sponsors, schools, educators and local volunteers to help them reach children.

We want guides to be available in children’s own languages, with companion videos and support for the adults playing alongside them.

Where we are today

A growing idea.

Wonder Why Kit is an initiative being developed with the intention of becoming a nonprofit. The formal setup is still being worked out; this site does not claim registered charity status.

As the organisation takes shape, we’ll publish its registration details, governance and how sponsorship funds will be used on this page.

The website supports the kits with ideas, guides and occasional playful companions. It is a place to find a spark, then carry it back into the physical world.

A comfortable place to play

Access & privacy.

This website has no accounts, advertising or analytics. The garden loads its 3D library from jsDelivr; that provider and the site host receive normal connection information.

The homepage respects your reduced-motion setting. The garden needs JavaScript and WebGL, and does not yet have a complete nonvisual equivalent. Its explanations remain readable if the 3D scene cannot load.

We’re still improving access across devices and ways of playing.
