Wonder Why Kit

Accessibility & privacy

A comfortable place to explore.

The activity uses labelled buttons, visible keyboard focus, live text messages, responsive controls and explanatory text/SVG illustrations. It needs a WebGL-capable browser to display the garden.

Known gaps: the 3D scene lacks a complete nonvisual equivalent; motion is not fully suppressible; messages may change quickly; colour contrast, touch usability, zoom and screen-reader behaviour still need formal review. We do not claim full WCAG conformance. A dedicated pause/reduced-animation option is a useful next improvement.

The current code has no account, analytics, advertising, microphone, storage or data-submission feature. It requests Three.js from jsDelivr, whose service receives normal connection metadata. The hosting provider also handles normal website requests. YouTube is linked only when a published ID is configured; visiting it invokes that service's policies. This is a technical description, not a legal compliance certification.

Avoid collecting children's identifying details in bug reports or play-test notes. Report device/browser and behaviour without names, faces or private classroom information.

Volunteering by email

The volunteer link opens your email app with contact@wonderwhykit.org as the recipient. Nothing is sent until you choose to send the email. Your email provider handles the message, and the recipient receives the details you include. Please do not include children’s personal information.
